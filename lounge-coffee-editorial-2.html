Our story

Coffee madewith patience,served withoutpretense.

LOUNGE started as a single roasting bench in the back of a small neighborhood shop. We were never interested in speed — only in getting the roast, the grind, and the pour right, every single time.

Today we still roast in small batches, work directly with growers we've known for years, and treat every cup as a chance to slow down. The café is built the same way: warm light, comfortable seats, and no rush to leave.

Maren Cole Founder & Head Roaster

We believe a great cup isn't rushed into existence — it's the result of a grower's patience, a roaster's attention, and a barista who cares whether your last sip tastes as good as your first.

Single-origin first Every bean is traceable to the farm it came from, and we roast each origin on its own schedule instead of blending for convenience.
Small batches, always We roast in runs of under 20kg so every bag reflects the same care as the one before it — never a factory run.
